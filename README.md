### 🧐 About Me

[cite_start]I am a **Computer Engineering** undergraduate at **Purdue University** (Minors in Stats & Math)[cite: 6, 7]. My research focuses on the intersection of AI Safety, linguistic steganography, and interpretability. I am currently working to understand how models hide information and how they react to being evaluated.

* 🔭 **Current Research:**
    * [cite_start]**CYNICS Lab:** Developing transformer-based linguistic steganography and decoding hidden watermarks in real-time[cite: 14, 16, 17].
    * [cite_start]**The LAB (Let All Build):** Mentoring research on "Attention Entropy" and analyzing reasoning collapse in logic tasks[cite: 19, 21].
* 🧪 **Previous Work:**
    * [cite_start]**Algoverse:** Created the *Probe-Rewrite-Evaluate* methodology to quantify "Evaluation Awareness" in LLMs[cite: 24, 26].
    * [cite_start]**RoBoat:** Led perception teams using YOLOv5 for autonomous maritime navigation[cite: 28, 30].
