## ⚡ About Me

[cite_start]I am a **Computer Engineering** undergraduate at **Purdue University** (Minors in Stats & Math)[cite: 7]. I specialize in **AI Safety**, **Interpretability**, and **Computer Vision**. My goal is to understand the "black box" of LLMs—specifically how they hide information and how they react to being tested.

* 🔭 **Current Work:**
    * [cite_start]**CYNICS Lab:** Developing transformer-based linguistic steganography pipelines and real-time watermark decoding[cite: 14, 16, 17].
    * [cite_start]**The LAB (Let All Build):** Leading research on "Attention Entropy" and reasoning collapse in non-standard dialects[cite: 19, 21].
* 🧪 **Previous Research:**
    * [cite_start]**Algoverse:** Developed the *Probe-Rewrite-Evaluate* framework to measure evaluation awareness in models[cite: 24, 26].
    * [cite_start]**RoBoat:** Led the perception team using YOLOv5 for autonomous maritime navigation[cite: 28, 30].
