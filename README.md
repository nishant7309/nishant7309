### 🌌 About Me
I am an AI Safety and Interpretability Researcher exploring the "why" behind Large Language Models. My work focuses on **evaluation awareness**, **linguistic steganography**, and **computer vision**. I believe AI is humanity's biggest bet, and I'm dedicated to making it safer and more understandable.

- 🔭 **Currently Researching:** Evaluation Awareness in LLMs at **CYNICS Lab @ Purdue**.
- 📝 **Recent Publications:** 
    - **NeurIPS 2025** (Workshop on Evaluation for Language Models)
    - **EMNLP 2025** (Wordplay Workshop)
- 🎓 **Education:** B.S. in Computer Engineering @ Purdue University (Minors in Stats & Math).
